## Decisions

- P0 is strictly non-behavioural. No production `src/*.ts` edits in this package;
  harness + docs only. This keeps the baseline-to-fix distinction auditable.
- New negative tests assert **current** behaviour with an explicit marker naming
  the invariant they will assert after the corresponding package lands. The suite
  must stay green at every commit (no unexplained red).
- Prime Directive applies: no new timeouts, spacing, capacity or cadence
  constants. Physical-parking work in P2 is a documented DCS proxy, cited as such.
- P5 requires a live DCS Studio bridge; if unavailable the epic stays open and the
  gate is recorded as pending, not passed.

### Findings that widen P1's scope beyond the epic plan's assumption

1. **Five paths bypass `task_board` entirely** — `cas_bai_sead.spawnSeadEscort`,
   `attack_waves.buildStrikePackage`'s inline escort, `reaction.spawnCap`,
   `reaction.spawn_bda`, `regen.spawnRegen`. A board-only contract will not cover them.
2. **Four builders spawn two groups per call** (SEAD, strike package, troop insert,

must come from `assign.c` / `reaction.c` / `ld_msgs.c`, not from internal-consistency reasoning.
They are P1/P3 inputs, not licence to "fix" the asymmetry.

   300 m; RUNWAY_TAKEOFF at t=445 s). Supports "unknown is not failed", and shows the 120 s
   diagnostics window is shorter than a real departure — it cannot prove a failure alone.

### The serious finding — backlog card 28

The bot reported that aircraft losses arrive as **CRASH (5)**, not **DEAD (8)**, and that
`regen.make_dead_handler` subscribes to DEAD only. **Lead verified this independently** against
the 219-event capture:

- CRASH ×32 — *all* aircraft (Heli ×23, SEAD ×3, OCAStrike ×3, Escort ×2, Recon ×1)
- DEAD ×39 — *zero* aircraft (ground columns, artillery, air defence, statics)

So **no shot-down aircraft has ever enqueued a regeneration replacement.** Aircraft attrition is
permanent and one-directional. This is a bigger economy bug than anything P3 fixed, and it
plausibly distorts every long-soak force-balance observation made to date. Filed as
`boards/project-backlog/28-aircraft-losses-never-regenerate.md` with the EECH `force.c` /
`hc_dstry.c` starting points. **Deliberately not fixed inside P3** — it is a behavioural economy
change needing its own EECH grounding, not a settlement fix.

### Lead decisions on P3's open items

- **Slot release on "all members resolved" — accepted.** A stuck member holding a slot is the
  lesser error than releasing a slot while aircraft are airborne. `unsettled_sorties()` gives
  visibility; P4 bounds it.
- **Credit landing ~30 s later — accepted.** Uses the pre-existing cleanup delay, no new
  constant. Crediting at touchdown is the duplicate-airframe hazard the epic forbids.
- **Four timer paths still outside the contract — P4, with the race written down.** A supply
  flight could be force-recycled by its backstop *and* credited if it lands home first.
- **`S._recycled` key spaces (unit vs group) — split in P4.** No collision possible today.
