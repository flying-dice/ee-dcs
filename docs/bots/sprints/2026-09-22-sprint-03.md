  repository's older `/mcp` configuration does not describe this installed bridge.

  Keep hot-start policy correction separate from the eventual causal fix.
- `isExist()` and `getSize()` include inactive placeholders. Require `isActive()`
  alongside life and airborne state before calling a spawn materialised.
- No retries, refunds, cadence changes or broad allocator work without causal
  evidence. Preserve concurrent checkout changes. No commit or push requested.
- The user's empty authored FARP supports a dynamic hot AH-64D pair: both active
  by +1 second and airborne by +120 seconds, with birth/takeoff events and visual
  confirmation. The same senior developer recycled the pair successfully at the
  same FARP, then reproduced disappearance with the identical aircraft spec at a
  fresh runtime FARP. Preserve the remaining location/initialization confounds in
  the ED report; do not claim a specific engine subsystem is proven faulty.
