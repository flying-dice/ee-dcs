# Decision 08 — Prefer site footprints over component buildings

OSM candidates are potential campaign spawn sites, not assertions that a particular building is a target. Put a site dot at the centre of its mapped landuse or facility footprint, using an on-footprint fallback for irregular geometry. Suppress component building or node dots of the same spawn kind within that site. Preserve all separately mapped site footprints, including nested industrial areas, and preserve different-kind candidates. This is source hierarchy, not a regional or proximity quota.
